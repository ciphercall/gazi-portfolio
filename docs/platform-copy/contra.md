## Positioning notes

- Contra shows project cards more than prose, so lead each card with the **outcome**, not the stack.
- Never list a project on Contra with a company you have not listed on the portfolio. Independent
  work is labelled independent on both.
- The strongest differentiator for Contra's audience is the enterprise + industrial mix: most
  marketplace developers have not shipped a PLC dashboard, and most industrial developers have not
  shipped a marketplace.

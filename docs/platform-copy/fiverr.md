## Pricing guardrails

- Do not sell "unlimited revisions". Cap at 2 rounds for Basic, 3 for Standard.
- For anything touching an ERP module, quote from a scope call, not from the gig tier.
- Keep delivery time honest: gigs that overpromise speed attract the wrong buyers.
